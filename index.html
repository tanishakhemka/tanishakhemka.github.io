About Me

My career took an unexpected turn when I realized code wasn't enough. I started as a software engineer, building applications and writing clean code. But when I landed my first data analyst position, everything changed - I learned SQL on the job and discovered that data wasn't just numbers, it was stories waiting to be told.

That realization shifted my path. I dove deeper into data analytics, building dashboards and predictive models. But I kept asking: what if this data could think and learn on its own?

That question led me to AI and eventually to Carnegie Mellon, where I'm now bridging the gap between cutting-edge machine learning research and real-world business applications. I build intelligent systems that understand context and make decisions—from NLP agents that process complex documents to AI solutions that transform how organizations work.
